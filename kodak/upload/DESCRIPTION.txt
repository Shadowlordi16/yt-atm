Kodak invented the digital camera in 1975. It studied the threat in 1981. By 2004 it was the No. 1 digital camera seller in America. In 2012 it filed for bankruptcy, the same year Facebook paid $1 billion for Instagram, a photo app with 13 employees.

Kodak didn't miss the future. It saw it clearly. It just couldn't make the future pay like the past.
