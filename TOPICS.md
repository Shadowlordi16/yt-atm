# Paper Empire: topic backlog

How a topic earns a slot:
1. **A name everyone knows**, so it gets searched.
2. **A twist**: the popular story vs. what happened.
3. **Sourceable**: SEC filings, court documents, major news.
4. **Free photos exist** on Wikimedia Commons or Openverse.

The "angle" column is a hypothesis to test in research, NOT a fact. If research doesn't support the twist, change the angle or drop the topic.
Each video's end card teases the next one, so keep the order or update the teaser.

## Rules for picking the next one
- Alternate the format: a long run of "falls" in a row feels repetitive. Every 3rd–4th video: a rise, a comeback or "how it makes money".
- A company in the news this month (a bankruptcy, a big sale, an anniversary) jumps the queue. A timely video gets more search traffic.
- Avoid stories every big channel has done to death (Theranos, FTX) unless the angle is genuinely new.
- One African business story for a global audience (e.g. Jumia, 9mobile) once the channel has traction.
