BlackBerry Was Winning. The Keyboard Was the Trap.

(alternatives to test later: BlackBerry's Keyboard Made It. Then It Killed It. · Why BlackBerry Couldn't Give Up Its Keyboard)

======== DESCRIPTION (copy everything between the lines) ========
In 2011, BlackBerry's parent company had its best year ever: almost 20 billion dollars in revenue, a 3.4 billion dollar profit, and 52 million phones sold. Four years AFTER the iPhone came out. Everyone says the iPhone killed BlackBerry. The numbers say the iPhone had not done it yet.

What killed BlackBerry was the thing that built it: the keyboard. The same physical keyboard that made it untouchable in the corporate world kept the screen small, the apps off the phone, and every fix years late.

This is the story of how the keyboard built BlackBerry, and then trapped it.
