**The twist:** everyone remembers the iPhone as the thing that killed BlackBerry. RIM's own numbers say something stranger: after the iPhone launched in 2007, BlackBerry kept growing for four more years, to record revenue of almost twenty billion dollars, profit of three point four billion, and eighty million users.
