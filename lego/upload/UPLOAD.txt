Lego Almost Went Bankrupt. Here's How It Saved Itself.

(alternatives to test later: Lego Was Dying. Then It Did Less. · How Lego Went From Almost Bankrupt to the World's Biggest Toy Company)

======== DESCRIPTION (copy everything between the lines) ========
In 2004, Lego lost almost 2 billion Danish kroner, the biggest loss in its history. One of its own executives later said it was almost bankrupt.

It had theme parks, video games, TV shows and action figures. What it had forgotten was the brick. Ten years later, it was the biggest toy company in the world.

This is the story of how Lego nearly lost everything, and saved itself by doing less.
