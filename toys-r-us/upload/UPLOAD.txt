Amazon Didn't Kill Toys "R" Us. A $6.6 Billion Deal Did.

(alternative to test later: The Deal That Buried Toys "R" Us)

======== DESCRIPTION (copy everything between the lines) ========
In March 2018, every Toys "R" Us store in America began to close: 735 stores, about 33,000 jobs. The headlines said Amazon killed it.

But Toys "R" Us once made Amazon its online store, then sued Amazon and won. What really buried it was a 2005 buyout that left the company paying around $400 million a year just to service its debt.

This is the story of the store every kid dreamed about, and the debt that buried it.
