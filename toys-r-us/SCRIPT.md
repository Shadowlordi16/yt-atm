## CHAPTER 3: THE BUYOUT

> In two thousand five, three investment firms bought Toys R Us: the private equity firms KKR and Bain Capital, and the real estate company Vornado. [S1]

> The price was about six point six billion dollars. [S1]

> But they didn't pay most of it themselves. They put in roughly one point three billion dollars of their own money, and borrowed the rest. [S1]

> Think of it like buying a house with a small deposit and a giant mortgage, and then making the house itself pay the mortgage.
