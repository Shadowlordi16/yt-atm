The Transfer That Broke Barcelona

(alternatives: Barcelona Paid £142M For Him. Four Years Later: £17M. · The £142M Hole In Barcelona's Accounts)

======== DESCRIPTION ========
Barcelona paid Liverpool £142 million for Philippe Coutinho in January 2018. Four years later, they sold him to Aston Villa for £17 million.

Everyone remembers the fee. Almost nobody remembers the accounting — and the accounting is the part that actually explains what happened next.

This is a football-money story, not a match recap. No broadcast footage: all original narration, tactical diagrams and licensed photos.
