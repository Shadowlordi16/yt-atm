In 2007, almost four in every ten phones sold on Earth were Nokias. Everyone says the iPhone killed it. But four years after the iPhone, Nokia was still the world's biggest phone maker.

Then, in a single morning in 2011, it announced that its own phone software was finished, before the replacement even existed.

This is how the world's biggest phone company burned its own platform. And how a company that started as a paper mill in 1865 survived it.
