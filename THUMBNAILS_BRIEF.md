# Thumbnail brief (for Codex or any image model)

Make a new thumbnail for each Paper Empire video below. **Two versions per video (A and B).** Save each as
`<video>/upload/thumb-new-A.jpg` and `thumb-new-B.jpg`: 1280×720 JPG, under 2 MB. Do not touch any other file.

## What's wrong with the current ones (fix this)
- Every thumbnail uses the same layout: a grey photo on the left, a black panel on the right, three cream words.
- The black panel disappears on YouTube's dark-mode background.
- The photos are flat grey, so nothing pops at small size.

## Rules
1. **One subject, filling the frame:** the product, the sign or the object. Shoot it close, with dramatic light and strong contrast. It must read at 160×90 px (phone feed size), so check that.
2. **2–4 words maximum**, huge, in a condensed bold font (League Gothic is in `logo/fonts/`), placed directly on the image. No panel and no paragraph.
3. **Colours:** a dark or newsprint base. **Red (#D7261E) for one thing only**, and only when it means a loss (a crack, a stamp, a falling line).
4. **No real people's faces generated by AI.** Don't create fake Stephen Elop, Knudstorp and so on. Objects, buildings, signs and products only.
5. **Nothing misleading.** No fake headlines, no fake quotes, nothing that didn't happen. The thumbnail must match the video's story.
6. **No logos of other brands** beyond the video's own company. No YouTube play buttons, arrows or emoji.
7. Each video should look different from the others: vary the layout and the subject's position.
