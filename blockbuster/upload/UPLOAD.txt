Blockbuster Almost Beat Netflix. Here's What Stopped It.

======== DESCRIPTION (copy everything between the lines) ========
In 2000, Blockbuster could have bought Netflix for $50 million. It said no. That's the story everyone tells.

But it isn't what happened. Blockbuster saw the future, built its own version of Netflix, and for a while it was catching up. Then a fight in its own boardroom changed everything.

This is how Blockbuster lost a war it was winning.
