# Brand — PAPER EMPIRE

## What it is
A faceless YouTube channel: business stories about famous companies. Main format: rise and fall. Also: rises (how X won), comebacks, and how X really makes money.
Long videos (10–20 min) plus Shorts cut from each.

## Audience
English-speaking viewers in the US, UK, Canada and Australia, 25–45, who like business, money and "what went wrong" stories.
Write for them: US spelling in titles and scripts, dollars, US/UK place names said plainly.

## The promise
Every video answers one question with a twist the viewer didn't expect
("Everyone blamed the shrimp. A 2014 real estate deal did the damage.").

## Voice
Narrator: Kokoro `am_adam` (American male), speed 1.0. Calm, dry, never hyped. No "smash that like button".

## The feeling
A serious financial newspaper with a sense of drama: facts printed in black, losses printed in red.

## Name
PAPER EMPIRE: companies built and burned. Wordmark in League Gothic caps, ink. Logo: see logo/ (concept chosen by the owner).

## Never
- An invented number, quote or date. Every figure on screen has a source line, and the sources go in the description.
- Allegations stated as fact: say "the filing alleges", and give the other side's answer.
- Mocking employees or customers. The story is about decisions, not people's jobs.
- Copyrighted footage beyond short commentary use. Prefer Wikimedia/CC photos, filings, and our own graphics.
